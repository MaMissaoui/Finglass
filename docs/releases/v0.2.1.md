Fixes and improvements after 0.2.0, from your feedback. Still a read-only, local-first app: nothing in Finglass can
move money.

**Alpha software.** Keep a backup of your data and of FinDesk's. This build is for macOS 11 or later only.

## What's new

- **Bank logos.** Accounts show their bank's logo on the Accounts page, in the Transactions account list and in the
  account properties: Sparkasse, Volksbank, DKB, HypoVereinsbank/UniCredit, Deutsche Bank, Commerzbank, Postbank,
  Santander and N26. Other banks get a coloured badge with their initials. The logos are part of the app; nothing is
  downloaded.
- **The bank card and its ⋮ menu as in FinDesk.** The menu floats over the page with icons: fetch history, include in
  "Sync all", rename (now for online banks too), connection settings and remove bank. The account count and total
  follow the bank name, with BLZ, BIC and the last sync on the line below.
- **Back.** After following a link, an arrow in the top bar (and ⌘[) takes you back. Back to the Dashboard reopens
  the month you left.

## Fixed

- **Dashboard links open the right month.** A category, the uncategorized slice and the open-tasks card open
  Transactions on this month (or on the month you're looking at), not over all time. The tax card opens Reports on
  that tax year.
- **Online sync is shown as not available yet.** Instead of a red error for each bank, the Accounts page shows one
  note and the sync buttons are disabled with an explanation. Import your bank's statements under Transactions until
  live sync arrives. FinTS URLs you entered are kept.
- **Type the passphrase straight away.** The unlock window focuses the passphrase field when it opens.
- **Reports:** clearer year arrows, and the header no longer jumps when the year changes.
- **Transactions account list:** rows no longer run into each other.

## Install

1. Download `Finglass_0.2.1_aarch64.dmg` below. `SHA256SUMS.txt` holds its checksum.
2. Open the DMG and drag Finglass into Applications, replacing 0.2.0. Your data stays where it is.
3. The app is ad-hoc signed, not notarized, so macOS blocks the first launch:
   1. Open Finglass once, then close the warning.
   2. Go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and choose **Open Anyway**.

OCR for images and scanned PDFs uses Tesseract and Poppler:

```sh
brew install tesseract tesseract-lang poppler
```
