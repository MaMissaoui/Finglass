Quieter controls and a fixed tax chart, from your feedback on 0.3.0. Still a read-only, local-first app: nothing in
Finglass can move money.

**Alpha software.** Keep a backup of your data and of FinDesk's. This build is for macOS 11 or later only.

## What's new

- **Filled, quiet buttons.** Buttons, toolbar icons and filter drop-downs are filled rounded rectangles without an
  outline. The main action and a switched-on toggle use the accent colour.
- **Month and year arrows.** The Dashboard's month and the Reports year sit between two round arrow buttons, with the
  period in large type. Click the month to pick another one.
- **Settings.** Larger rows with icons, the selected section in a soft accent tint, and each section opening with a
  title and a short explanation. Darstellung is a form with its labels in one column.
- **Transactions.** The search field and the filters share one line when there is room, and move to two lines when
  there isn't. The account list has taller rows and more space between groups; "last fetched" moved to the tooltip.
- **Easier to read.** Small text is 13 px instead of 12 px.

## Fixed

- **Dashboard → tax-deductible expenses:** the donut drew its slices as crossing strokes with a grey bar behind the
  total. The slices now sit on the ring.

## Install

Double-click `install-mac.command` from a checkout to download and install this release, or:

1. Download `Finglass_0.3.1_aarch64.dmg` below. `SHA256SUMS.txt` holds its checksum.
2. Open the DMG and drag Finglass into Applications, replacing 0.3.0. Your data stays where it is.
3. The app is ad-hoc signed, not notarized, so macOS blocks the first launch:
   1. Open Finglass once, then close the warning.
   2. Go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and choose **Open Anyway**.

OCR for images and scanned PDFs uses Tesseract and Poppler:

```sh
brew install tesseract tesseract-lang poppler
```
