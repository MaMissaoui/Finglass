The second alpha of Finglass. It looks and behaves like FinDesk, screen by screen, while staying a read-only,
local-first app: nothing in Finglass can move money.

**Alpha software.** Keep a backup of your data and of FinDesk's. This build is for macOS 11 or later only.

## What's new

- **One click is enough.** Buttons, tabs, chips, menu entries and sidebar items act on the first click. Before, the
  first click often only took the keyboard focus.
- **The Dashboard opens on the current month**, every time you open it, in your local time zone. A month drop-down
  next to the arrows jumps to any month with bookings.
- **A native macOS menu bar** with File, View, Window and Help, and keyboard shortcuts: ⌘N new transaction, ⌘⇧I import
  a statement, ⌘, Settings, ⌘1–⌘6 for the pages, ⌘⇧D dark theme.
- **FinDesk's look:**
  - IBM Plex Sans, FinDesk's type sizes and its filled Material icons.
  - The language selector shows a flag and lists the languages with their flags.
  - Rounded buttons with icons and segmented buttons.
  - The Transactions filters in one row of drop-downs.
  - Dots, badges and amounts centred in every list.
- **Settings** shows a section list on the left and one section at a time. "Open settings" and "Manage profiles…" go
  straight to their section.
- **Reports:** one Export button with a menu, the year next to its arrows, and aligned tables.
- **Forms and dialogs:**
  - Text fields with floating labels, drop-downs and check boxes as in FinDesk.
  - Dialogs with FinDesk's titles and button order.
  - An unlock window that shows the profile as a chip.
- **Small improvements:**
  - The main window opens maximized, as FinDesk's.
  - ⌘F jumps to the search on Transactions and Documents.
  - Every icon-only button has a tooltip.
  - Clickable rows highlight under the pointer.

## Not in this release

- **Live bank sync (FinTS).** You can add your bank and its login, but a sync still ends with "not available yet".
  Until then, import your bank's statement files.
- Windows and Linux builds.
- A Developer ID signature and notarization.

## Install

1. Download `Finglass_0.2.0_aarch64.dmg` below. `SHA256SUMS.txt` holds its checksum.
2. Open the DMG and drag Finglass into Applications, replacing 0.1.0. Your data stays where it is.
3. The app is ad-hoc signed, not notarized, so macOS blocks the first launch:
   1. Open Finglass once, then close the warning.
   2. Go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and choose **Open Anyway**.

OCR for images and scanned PDFs uses Tesseract and Poppler:

```sh
brew install tesseract tesseract-lang poppler
```

PDFs with a text layer need neither.

Fonts: IBM Plex Sans (SIL Open Font License 1.1). Icons: Material icons (Apache-2.0). Bank codes: Quelle: Deutsche
Bundesbank.
