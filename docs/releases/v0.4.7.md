Fixes from a review of every screen, plus groundwork for Linux. Still read-only and local-first: nothing in Finglass can
move money.

**Alpha software.** Keep a backup of your data. This build is for macOS 13 or later only.

## Fixed

- **Balance history starts at the account's first booking.** With "This & last year" the chart began on January 1 of
  last year even for an account whose bookings start later, so most of it was a flat line. Accounts with older
  history still start at the beginning of the period.
- The balance chart's labels no longer cover the line or each other.
- **Documents:** a linked booking shows its date as 07.10.2026 instead of 2026-10-07, and a booking without partner or
  purpose is called "Buchung" / "Booking" / "Opération" instead of "Transaction" in every language.
- **Settings → Backup & restore:** the bank list's date shows as 14.11.2026, and the bank list has its own block.
- Booking details: "Not tax relevant" lines up with the Tax control.
- Reports → Recurring: the column above "Monthly" is called "Rhythmus" / "Interval" / "Fréquence".

## Please test

1. Transactions → pick one account: the balance history starts at its first booking, not on 01.01.2025.
2. Documents: a linked document shows the booking date as dd.mm.yyyy.
3. Settings → Backup & restore: the bank list shows the date it was loaded as dd.mm.yyyy.

## For developers

- Linux "Remember PIN" (not released): the Secret Service store keeps one bus connection, a failed check waits at most
  one deadline, and it never falls back to an unencrypted session after a timeout. Forgetting a profile's PINs no longer
  depends on the availability check.
