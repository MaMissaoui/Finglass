Small usability fixes on Accounts, Transactions and Reports. Still read-only and local-first: nothing in Finglass can
move money.

**Alpha software.** Keep a backup of your data. This build is for macOS 13 or later only.

## New

- **Transactions: "This & last year".** A new period from January 1 of last year through December 31 of this year.
  Transactions now opens with it instead of "This month". Links from the Dashboard and Reports keep their own period.

## Changed

- **Reports opens on Monthly breakdown.** The tabs are now Monthly breakdown, Category analysis, Recurring, Tax
  summary and Tax bookings. A link to a tax year still opens Tax bookings.

## Fixed

- **Accounts: one menu at a time.** Opening a bank's or an account's menu closes any other open menu. A click
  elsewhere, Escape, scrolling or resizing the window closes it. The bank menu opens below its button, or above it
  when there is no room, and scrolls instead of being cut off.

## Please test

1. Accounts: open the menu of one bank, then of another. Only the second stays open. The last bank's menu opens upward
   and is fully visible.
2. Transactions opens with "This & last year" and shows last year's bookings too.
3. Reports opens on Monthly breakdown. The tax tabs are at the right.
