Up-to-date dependencies and continuous security checks. No change to what Finglass does. Still read-only and
local-first: nothing in Finglass can move money.

**Alpha software.** Keep a backup of your data. This build is for macOS 13 or later only.

## Security

- All dependencies are current. No known vulnerability affects this build: `govulncheck` checked the source and the
  finished app binary (Go 1.27.4), and `npm audit` reports none.
- From now on, known vulnerabilities are fixed as soon as they are known (see SECURITY.md):
  - Dependabot watches the Go modules, the frontend packages and the build actions;
  - a daily scan runs on macOS, Linux and Windows;
  - every release binary is scanned before it is published.

## Changed

- The frontend toolchain was updated (Svelte 5.59, Vite 8.3.8, ESLint 10.15, Prettier 3.10). Screens look the same.
