A new look for Finglass: calmer, with the precision of a bank statement. Still a read-only, local-first app: nothing
in Finglass can move money.

**Alpha software.** Keep a backup of your data and of FinDesk's. This build is for macOS 11 or later only.

## What's new

- **A new look, everywhere.** Ink-blue text on white, one petrol accent, and colour only where it means something:
  green for money coming in, red only for negative balances, errors and destructive actions. Expenses are no longer
  red. Selected rows and pages are marked with a neutral fill and a thin petrol bar. Corners are tighter, cards no
  longer cast shadows, and page titles and key figures are calmer. Dark mode follows the same rules.
- **The Dashboard opens with the month's statement.** Like the foot of a bank statement: carried over from last
  month, income, expenses, transfers and loans, a rule, then what's available. The column adds up exactly for the
  shown month, in your main currency; loans and other currencies are noted below it. Open tasks sit next to it, one
  click from each list.
- **Page details:**
  - Accounts: no greyed-out Sync buttons while online sync isn't available; the note explains it.
  - Transactions: a wider account panel on large windows, and "Select all" moved into the table header.
  - Reports: share bars in the tax summary, and the figures on one band.
  - Settings: each rule is titled by the category it sets.
  - The profile selector fits its name.
- **The Mac installer installs the latest release.** `install-mac.command` downloads the newest release, checks its
  checksum and signature, and replaces the app safely; no Rust needed. `--from-source` still builds the checkout.

## Install

1. Download `Finglass_0.3.0_aarch64.dmg` below. `SHA256SUMS.txt` holds its checksum. Or double-click
   `install-mac.command` from a checkout.
2. Open the DMG and drag Finglass into Applications, replacing 0.2.2. Your data stays where it is.
3. The app is ad-hoc signed, not notarized, so macOS blocks the first launch:
   1. Open Finglass once, then close the warning.
   2. Go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and choose **Open Anyway**.

OCR for images and scanned PDFs uses Tesseract and Poppler:

```sh
brew install tesseract tesseract-lang poppler
```
