Clearer charts: a new set of category colours, the tax card as a split bar, and a fix for the budget meters. Still a
read-only, local-first app: nothing in Finglass can move money.

**Alpha software.** Keep a backup of your data and of FinDesk's. This build is for macOS 11 or later only.

## Changed

- **Category colours:** a new set of 24 default colours (six hues in four tones), checked so that neighbouring
  colours stay apart for colour-blind readers too, in light and dark mode. Green and red are no longer category
  colours, because they mean money in and alerts. Your existing categories keep their colours; new ones and the
  colour picker use the new set.
- **Dashboard → tax-deductible card:** the donut is now the same split bar as the expenses card, with the year's
  total above it and one row per category.
- **Dashboard → income vs expenses:** narrower bars with square bases; hover a month to see its income and
  expenses. The zero on the axis now reads `€0` like the other labels.
- **Balance charts** (Transactions, account detail): a dot marks the latest balance, labelled with its amount.

## Fixed

- **Dashboard → budgets:** the fill now starts at the left of the track (it was centred), and shows state instead
  of the category colour: petrol normally, amber when over or ahead of the month.

## Install

Double-click `install-mac.command` from a checkout to download and install this release, or:

1. Download `Finglass_0.3.4_aarch64.dmg` below. `SHA256SUMS.txt` holds its checksum.
2. Open the DMG and drag Finglass into Applications, replacing 0.3.3. Your data stays where it is.
3. The app is ad-hoc signed, not notarized, so macOS blocks the first launch:
   1. Open Finglass once, then close the warning.
   2. Go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and choose **Open Anyway**.

OCR for images and scanned PDFs uses Tesseract and Poppler:

```sh
brew install tesseract tesseract-lang poppler
```
