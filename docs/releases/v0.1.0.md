The first alpha of Finglass: a read-only, local-first view of your finances, rebuilt in Rust from
[FinDesk](https://github.com/MaMissaoui/FinDesk) without any payment features. Nothing in Finglass can move money.

**Alpha software.** Keep a backup of your data and of FinDesk's. This build is for macOS 11 or later only.

## What works

- **Encrypted profiles:** an encrypted database (SQLCipher) per profile, unlocked with your passphrase. Create, rename,
  delete and switch profiles while the app runs.
- **Accounts:** manual accounts with IBAN check, currencies, account types, groups and the account panel.
- **Bank file imports:** CSV (bank presets and detection), MT940, camt.052/053, OFX, QIF and XLSX, with a preview and
  duplicate detection.
- **Transactions:** filters, details, categories, rules and auto-categorisation.
- **Dashboard:** balances, income and expenses, expenses by category and budgets, in every currency you hold, without
  conversion.
- **Documents:** receipts per profile, with PDF text extraction and OCR, and matching to bookings.
- **Reports:** categories, recurring costs, cost per year and month, tax groups and missing receipts, exported as PDF
  and XLSX.
- **Backups:** backup and restore of the encrypted database and the documents.
- **Moving from FinDesk:**
  - On first start, Finglass offers to migrate every FinDesk profile on this Mac. It only reads FinDesk's data.
  - Settings also has a one-profile import.
  - Payment orders and remembered PINs are not copied.
- **Interface:** English, German and French; light and dark theme.

## Not in this release

- **Live bank sync (FinTS).** You can add your bank and its login, and look up your bank by name, BLZ, BIC or IBAN in
  the Bundesbank's bank code file. A sync still ends with "not available yet". The FinTS connection comes in a later
  release. Until then, import your bank's statement files.
- Windows and Linux builds.
- A Developer ID signature and notarization.

## Install

1. Download `Finglass_0.1.0_aarch64.dmg` below. `SHA256SUMS.txt` holds its checksum.
2. Open the DMG and drag Finglass into Applications.
3. The app is ad-hoc signed, not notarized, so macOS blocks the first launch:
   1. Open Finglass once, then close the warning.
   2. Go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and choose **Open Anyway**.

OCR for images and scanned PDFs uses Tesseract and Poppler:

```sh
brew install tesseract tesseract-lang poppler
```

PDFs with a text layer need neither.

Bank codes: Quelle: Deutsche Bundesbank.
