The first Finglass build that syncs with your banks. Still read-only and local-first: nothing in Finglass can move
money.

**Alpha software.** Keep a backup of your data. This build is for macOS 13 or later only.

## New

- **Online sync, bank by bank.** A bank can sync once its last **Test connection** reported **GO**. A NO-GO from the
  bank, or a change of bank code, user id or server, locks it again until the next GO.
  - **Sync now** in the bank menu (right after Test connection) syncs that bank, whether or not it is in Sync all.
  - **Include in Sync all** can always be switched on or off — for bank maintenance days or when your device for the
    TAN isn't at hand.
  - **Sync all** syncs the included banks that are ready and names the ones it skipped.
  - **Remember PIN for syncs** (optional, per bank): the PIN goes into the macOS Keychain only after a successful
    sync, and is removed when the bank rejects it, when the login changes and when you remove the bank.
  - A bank that reached GO only through the HypoVereinsbank second try (0.4.1) stays locked for now: sync can't do
    that new registration yet. The Test connection result says so.
- **Onboarding:** the FinDesk migration asks you to confirm "nobody can recover my data" on the passphrase step, not
  before; a click without the tick keeps what you typed. The passphrase rule sits under the field and counts as you
  type ("4 of 10 characters").

## Changed

- Clearer fields and checkboxes (stronger edges), and animations stop when macOS "Reduce motion" is on.
- One main currency per figure: further currencies sit on a small line below ("+ $35.97").
- Reports: tax-relevant expenses use the same category rows as the Dashboard; Export sits next to the period.
- Documents: Open is the main action; Delete is a quiet icon (still asks first).
- The sidebar collapses by itself in narrow windows.
- Accessibility: rows in Accounts and Transactions work better with VoiceOver; an automated check guards it.
- A sync never sends your PIN twice in a row after the bank answers with a security code.

## Install

1. Download `Finglass_0.4.2_universal.dmg` below. `SHA256SUMS.txt` holds its checksum.
2. Quit Finglass, open the DMG and drag Finglass into Applications, replacing the older version. Your data stays as it
   is.
3. The app is ad-hoc signed, not notarized: if macOS blocks the first launch, go to **System Settings → Privacy &
   Security** and choose **Open Anyway**.
